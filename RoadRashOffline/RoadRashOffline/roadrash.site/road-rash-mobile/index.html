Road Rash mobile browser guide

By Rustyt Gaming · Updated 28 October 2026

You can play the browser version of Road Rash on a phone using on-screen controls. Open roadrash.site in your browser, choose Race Now and select a track. This guide covers the mobile layout; it is not an APK download.

Start on Android or iPhone

  1. Open roadrash.site in a current mobile browser, such as Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, with JavaScript enabled.
  2. Let the menu load, choose Race Now and select a course. Wait for the track assets to finish loading.
  3. Hold the phone sideways for the most natural view of the landscape game and its controls.

There is no installer or plugin required for browser play. Actual performance depends on your phone and browser. An internet connection is needed to load the game and to race with friends; this is not a guaranteed offline app.

Landscape play, even when the phone is upright

The game uses a landscape layout. If you hold a phone upright, the game and its controls rotate sideways within the page so play can continue. Turning the phone sideways makes the screen and buttons easier to use. This does not depend on the browser granting an orientation lock.

Steering, acceleration and braking

Default mobile race controls
ControlWhat it does
◀ / ▶Hold a direction to steer left or right.
ACCELHold to accelerate manually.
BRAKEHold to slow down. Braking overrides AUTO while held.
AUTOToggle auto acceleration. It starts on for each race and stays on through a crash or pause until switched off.
PUNCH / KICKAttack a rider beside you with a fist or foot.
SWINGUse your equipped weapon. This touch button is disabled when unarmed.

Attacks choose the opponent's side automatically. Hold an attack button to repeat, but keep your bike alongside the rival: an animation alone does not mean the attack connected. See attack range and weapon controls.

Move the buttons or change steering

Use the gear button at the top of the race screen to move, resize or adjust the transparency of touch controls. The layout choices include Default, Left-handed and Classic. Your selected layout may differ from the default controls described above.

The pause menu also offers joystick and tilt steering. With joystick steering selected, drag left or right to steer and push up to accelerate. Tilt availability can depend on your device, browser and motion permissions.

Race friends from a phone

Choose Play with Friends from the main menu. Share the room invite or join with a room code. Each rider uses their own device, and everyone needs a working connection. Follow the room creation and joining guide for details.

Common mobile questions

Do I need to install an APK?

No APK is needed to play this browser version. The play link opens the website, not an app store or installer.

Why did the sound not start immediately?

A mobile browser may wait for a tap before allowing audio. Start the game and check the in-game sound settings if it is quiet.

Where should I check my progress?

Return using the same browser and check Career or the profile under Leaderboard. The game keeps local progress and can sync through its rider account service. Clearing site data can affect access; see the saving and account information.
