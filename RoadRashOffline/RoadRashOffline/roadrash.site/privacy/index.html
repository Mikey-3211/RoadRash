Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

RoadRash Classic (roadrash.site) is a free, unofficial fan site by Rustyt Gaming where you can play a browser version of the classic Road Rash game. Each browser automatically gets its own rider account, so your progress and race times are kept without signing up. You can optionally register a username and password to access your rider on other devices. We do not ask for your real name or email address.

Google Analytics

We keep game-event records on our server for 30 days to understand visits, races, multiplayer usage and support choices. They contain the event, time, country and limited game details. For Buy Me a Coffee and its support options, we also store your IP address and the approximate city our server looks up for it. We do not store an IP address or city for other game events, and do not record account IDs, cookies, names, chat, invite codes, payment amounts or whether a payment happened. These records follow your analytics choice. Records follow the current analytics setting. Counts may be incomplete.

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and play the game, for example how many races are started and finished, so we can fix problems and improve it. Analytics is enabled by default without an automatic cookie banner; a saved opt-out is respected.

  • When it is on (the default, unless you turn it off): Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device type, your approximate location (country or city, worked out from your IP address), how you arrived at the site, and game events: which menu option you chose, when a race starts and ends, the track and level, your finishing place and time, and how long a race took to load.
  • If you turn it off: no analytics cookies are set. Using Google's Consent Mode, the site may still send Google basic signals without cookies or identifiers (for example that a page was viewed), which Google uses only to estimate overall visitor numbers.
  • We never use the data for advertising, we don't show ads, and we don't sell or share it with anyone other than Google as our analytics provider.
  • Google processes this data on our behalf and may process it outside your country, including in the United States. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • We keep analytics data for at most 14 months.

Playing with friends

When you use Play with Friends, the player name you type, your chosen rider, your race positions and any chat messages are passed through our server to the other players in your room, as the race happens. They are kept in the server's memory only while the room is open. They are not saved or logged, and they are gone when the room closes. Your IP address is used only to make the connection work. Don't put personal information in your player name or chat.

Chat and private messages

The Global section of Chat is public. We store those messages, their author names and timestamps on our server so players can read earlier conversations. Private messages can be sent between accepted friends. The authenticated site owner, rustyt, may also contact players without a friendship. Messages are accessible in the game only to their two participants. Suggestions submitted through the “Make RoadRash better” poll are also delivered privately to rustyt, even without a friendship. We retain poll suggestions separately as feedback, including after account deletion. We store private message text, participants, timestamps and read positions on our server so conversations and unread counts continue across visits and devices. The same chat filters apply to both sections.

Chat messages have no automatic expiry. Removing a friend stops new private messages but keeps the conversation history. Account deletion removes that account's private conversations. We also store friend requests and accepted friendships; clearing browser storage does not delete these records. Contact the site owner with your Player ID to request account deletion. Avoid sending sensitive personal information in chat.

Visible game pages send short-lived presence updates for online dots and the online-player total. Only recent updates contribute to that total, game AI is excluded, and multiple tabs or devices using the same account count once.

Rider accounts and leaderboards

Your browser's rider account has a Player ID (such as Rider_K7Q2XM) and a leaderboard name, which is generated for you and can be changed on the Leaderboard page. PostgreSQL stores the account's creation and last-visit times, your game profile, race checkpoints and completed races (result, place, time, hits and falls), and a one-way hash of your browser's device secret. Optional login passwords are stored as plain text, not password hashes; database copies and backups contain those passwords. Use a password you do not use elsewhere. Passwords are not included in public profiles, API responses or application logs. Redis keeps short-lived ranking caches that can be rebuilt from the database.

Approximate location. To show city and country leaderboards, our server looks up the approximate city and country of your IP address when the game connects (IP geolocation by DB-IP; a nearby suburb counts as its large city, from GeoNames data), and stores only that city name and country code with your account. Your IP address itself is not stored with your account. Your leaderboard name, best times and country flag are public on the leaderboards, and appear under your city and country. Don't put personal information in your leaderboard name.

Expired sessions are removed automatically. Finished races keep their result; their full checkpoint is cleared. Account records and results remain until you request deletion from the site owner. Clearing your browser's site data stops this browser finding the account, but does not delete it; quote your Player ID (shown under Leaderboard → Your profile) if you want it restored or deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can accept or decline analytics at any time with Change cookie settings above. Declining removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Contact the site owner with your Player ID to request access to, correction of, restoration of or deletion of your rider account data. Analytics does not receive your account identifier or session cookie.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If we change how the site uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For questions about this policy or your data, contact the site owner through roadrash.site.
