VIRTUA COP 2

3D LIGHTGUN ARCADE SHOOTER

Originally developed by SEGA AM2 in 1995/1997, Virtua Cop 2 revolutionized 3D lightgun shooters with high-octane pursuits, dynamic camera angles, and branching paths across the city.

DESKTOP CONTROLS

  • Mouse: Aim crosshairs
  • Left Click: Fire weapon
  • Right Click: Reload cylinder
  • P or Middle Click: Pause / Start
  • ESC: Release mouse capture

MOBILE CONTROLS

  • Orientation: Auto-rotates to Landscape
  • Tap Target: Aim & fire instantly
  • Reload Button: Large on-screen cylinder refill
  • PWA: Installable to home screen
